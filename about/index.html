ITGT/About

A global strategy and transformation advisory firm

ITGT Consultancy is a global strategy and transformation advisory firm, enabling business and government leaders across İstanbul, Baku, Riyadh, and Singapore to navigate disruption, accelerate enterprise reinvention, and achieve sustainable competitive advantage.

We partner with C-suites and boards to architect bold strategies, modernize technology governance, and embed resilience at the core of operations. With deep domain expertise in corporate strategy, IT governance, risk, and regulatory compliance, our integrated approach bridges vision with execution.

By aligning enterprise ambition with technology-enabled capabilities, ITGT drives end-to-end transformation—unlocking value, managing risk at scale, and delivering measurable results in complex, fast-evolving environments.
