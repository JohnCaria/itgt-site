Digital Transformation and Enterprise Agility Consulting

We turn transformation programs from slogans into a managed portfolio.

AI Governance IT GRC IT Strategy Data Governance PMO & ITSM Transformation

Most transformation programs do not fail for technical reasons. They stop because scope expands, benefit is not measured and people keep working the old way.

Our job is to establish the three things that keep a program standing: a clear scope boundary, a measurable definition of benefit and a governance rhythm that carries the change.

How we work

  • Transformation diagnosis and readiness assessment
  • Portfolio design and wave planning
  • Benefit realization framework
  • Change and communication plan
  • Program governance and decision rhythm set-up

Tangible deliverables

  • Transformation diagnosis report
  • Initiative portfolio and wave plan
  • Benefit tree and measurement model
  • Change management plan
  • Program governance structure
  • Board progress scorecard

Frequently asked questions

Can agility be applied to the whole organization?

No, and when it is attempted it usually does harm. Agility works where uncertainty is high. In predictable and regulated work, classic discipline is safer.

How do you measure benefit?

By recording the baseline before the program starts. The benefit of a program whose baseline was never measured cannot be proven afterwards, so measurement is the work of the first week.
