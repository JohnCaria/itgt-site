AI Governance Consulting

We take AI from pilot project to a managed enterprise capability. ISO 42001, the EU AI Act and board reporting in a single operating model. Built on our own framework, AI-GMM.

AI Governance IT GRC IT Strategy Data Governance PMO & ITSM Transformation

In most companies the AI problem is no longer a technology problem. Models work, pilots are completed, teams are excited. Then the board asks a single question: which of these systems makes the decision, which one makes a recommendation, and who is accountable when one of them decides wrongly.

The answer to that question is not inside a model. It is inside an operating model.

AI governance is the structure that defines who approves AI in the organization, which risk is accepted, at which threshold it stops, and how all of this is evidenced. It is not a policy text, it is a working decision mechanism.

Five questions the board asks

  • How many AI systems are running in the organization right now, and who holds the inventory
  • Which of these systems are considered high risk, and who made that classification
  • What process kicks in when a model produces an unexpected output
  • Where does the boundary of responsibility begin when using third-party models
  • Could you show all of this to an auditor within three days

Our approach: AI-GMM

The ITGT AI Governance Maturity Model is our own framework, working across six domains and five maturity levels. It is not a generic checklist; it measures where the organization stands and derives the shortest path to the next level.

Six domains: strategy and ownership, risk and compliance, data foundation, model lifecycle, human oversight, value measurement.

Five levels: ad hoc, aware, defined, managed, optimized.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between AI governance and AI strategy?

Strategy tells you where you are going; governance tells you who approves what on the way there. Organizations with a strategy but no governance start fast and stop at the first serious incident.

Can governance be established without ISO 42001 certification?

It can, and in most cases governance comes first. The certificate is the external validation of an established system. Without the system, certification turns into filling an empty file.

Why does the EU AI Act concern a company in Türkiye?

The regulation also covers systems whose output is used within the European Union. Turkish companies that serve Europe, belong to a European group, or produce AI-supported output for a customer in Europe are in scope.

How long does the work take?

Diagnosis takes two weeks. Setting up the governance structure and getting the first report to the board typically takes three months.
