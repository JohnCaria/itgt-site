AI Maturity Test: where does your organization stand?

AI maturity is not a feeling, it is a measurable state. This tool scores where your organization stands across six domains, compares it with the sector average and produces a 90-day plan to reach the next level. It takes ten minutes and it is free.

Start the test
What it measures

The ITGT AI Governance Maturity Model (AI-GMM) assesses AI governance across six domains:

01
Strategy and ownership

Who holds AI decisions and how they connect to business strategy

02
Risk and compliance

Risk classification, ISO 42001 and EU AI Act readiness

03
Data foundation

The source, quality and management of training data

04
Model lifecycle

Discipline in development, testing, deployment and monitoring

05
Human oversight

When a decision returns to a human and the intervention mechanism

06
Value measurement

Tracking how AI investment turns into business results

Five maturity levels

Level 1
Ad hoc

AI is in use but there is no inventory; decisions are made unit by unit.

Level 2
Aware

Risks are discussed, ownership is partly defined, there is no written rule yet.

Level 3
Defined

Policy, inventory and an approval process exist; implementation is inconsistent.

Level 4
Managed

Controls work, reporting is regular, audit-ready evidence is produced.

Level 5
Optimized

Governance increases decision speed; measurement turns into improvement.

What you get

  • Maturity score across six domains and a radar chart
  • A heat map showing weak areas
  • Sector comparison
  • A 90-day priority plan

Frequently asked questions

Who should take the test?

Someone with a holistic view of AI use in the organization: the CIO, CTO, risk manager or transformation lead. A single team's perspective skews the result.

Are results shared?

No. Your score stays private to you; the comparison is calculated from anonymous aggregate data.

What happens after the diagnostic?

You receive your report. If you wish, we interpret the results together in a conversation; if you prefer, you work with your own team. The diagnostic is not a sales step, it is a starting point.
